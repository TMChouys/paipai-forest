==============================================================================

mesh, skeleton, 2048x2048 baked color texture (JPEG inside the file) and animation clips.
preview.png shows all 18 models on one sheet.

Animation clips (standard glTF animations, linear keys, 30 fps; fly clips 60 fps)
  idle      breathing, small head/tail motion, loop
  walk      walk cycle, loop, in place
  gesture   short greeting wiggle, play once
  swimIdle  floating in water, loop
  swim      swimming cycle, loop, in place
  fly       hovering flight with wing flaps, loop
All clips are in place (no root motion): move the object yourself while walk, swim or fly plays.
Otter, Axolotl and Capybara have both land (idle, walk, gesture) and water (swimIdle, swim) clips;
Turtle and Seal are water only; Hummingbird and Parrot fly only.

Technical notes
  - Scale: every model is about 1.7 units tall (Meshy normalizes its exports). Scale to taste.
  - Y up, models face +Z (standard glTF).
  - Skeleton: the Meshy (UniRig) auto-rig exactly as exported, bones are named Bone_001 and so on.
    The clips were generated procedurally: bone roles (hips, spine, neck, head, ears, legs, wings, tail)
    were derived from the rest pose, then the motion was baked into ordinary glTF animation channels,
    so it plays in Blender, Godot, Unity, Unreal, three.js and Babylon.js without extra code.
  - Material: PBR metallic-roughness, base color texture only (metallic 0, roughness 1), double sided.
  - Built with Babylon.js and Node.js. Texture JPEG quality 92.
